LibriQ

A private reading tracker

Most book apps are part reading log, part social network. Your shelves become a profile, your ratings feed a recommendation engine, and the home screen fills up with what other people are reading. LibriQ is only the reading log. It is for keeping track of your own books, at your own pace.

What you won't find

  • No ads. Nothing in the app is paid placement.
  • No social feed. There are no followers, likes or friend activity.
  • No public profile. Your library, ratings and notes are not published anywhere.
  • No data sales. Your reading history is not sold or shared for marketing.

Page views are counted with cookieless analytics that don't identify you. The Privacy Policy lists exactly what is stored and which services process it.

What it does

  • Track each book as Reading, Want to Read, Finished or Did Not Finish, with your current page.
  • Rate books, write private notes, and keep quotes and Kindle highlights.
  • Set a yearly reading goal, keep a daily streak, and see pages read per month, top genres and authors.
  • Get recommendations based on the books already in your library.

Why it asks for an account

An account lets LibriQ keep your library in sync between your phone and your computer and back it up if a device is lost. You can sign up with an email address or with Google. Once installed as an app, LibriQ keeps working offline and syncs when you are back online.

Your data stays yours

You can export your whole library as a JSON file from Settings at any time. Deleting your library or your account removes it from your devices and from our servers. You don't need to email anyone to ask.

Switching from another app

If you are coming from Goodreads, you can import your Goodreads library with your shelves, ratings and reviews. Kindle readers can import their highlights from My Clippings.txt.
