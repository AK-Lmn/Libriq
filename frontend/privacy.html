LibriQ

Privacy Policy

Last updated November 26, 2026

LibriQ is a personal reading tracker. Your library is yours: we don't sell it, show ads, or publish it anywhere. This page lists what we store, who handles it for us, and how to take it with you or delete it.

What we store

  • Your account. Your email address, display name, and sign-in method (email and password, or Google). Passwords are handled by Firebase Authentication; we never see them.
  • Your library. Books, reading status and progress, ratings, notes, quotes, goals, streaks, activity, and profile settings. These are saved on your device and, when you're signed in, in your account so they sync between devices.
  • Backups. If you use cloud backup, a copy of your library is stored in your account.
  • Imports. Goodreads and Kindle files are read on your device. Only the books and quotes you choose to import are saved.

Who processes it

  • Firebase (Google): sign-in, and storage of your account data and library.
  • Vercel: hosts the app. Its servers keep standard request logs, such as IP address and browser type, for security and reliability.
  • Vercel Web Analytics: counts page views without cookies and without identifying you.
  • Resend: sends account emails, the weekly reading email if you turn it on, and occasional product announcements, and receives mail sent to our support address.
  • Book data services: when you search, the search text is sent to Google Books, Open Library, Gutendex, Internet Archive, and Wikipedia to find books and covers. They don't receive your account or library.

These providers may process data in the United States and other countries.

Cookies and tracking

LibriQ doesn't use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies. The app stores your library and settings in your browser's local storage so it works offline. Firebase may store a sign-in token so you stay signed in.

Emails

We email you about your account, such as password resets and email changes, and occasionally about important changes to LibriQ. To stop announcement emails, reply to one or write to support@libriq.app.

If you turn on the weekly reading email in Settings, we send you a short summary each Monday, built from the library data Account Sync has stored for you: books you finished that week, books you are reading, books you added, one quote you saved, how many notes you updated (never their text), your streak and your goal progress. It uses the name from your LibriQ profile. It is off by default. You can turn it off in Settings, or with the unsubscribe link in any weekly email.

Your choices

  • Export. Download your whole library from Settings at any time.
  • Turn off sync. Account Sync can be switched off in Settings.
  • Delete your account. Settings › Delete account removes your account and the library, backups, and sync data stored with it. Data left in a browser you used stays there until you clear it.
  • Ask us. To get a copy of your data, correct it, or delete it, email support@libriq.app.

How long we keep it

We keep your account data until you delete your account or ask us to. Server logs are kept by Vercel for a limited time under its own policy.

Children

LibriQ isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data. If you think a child has created an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we'll update the date above and tell you in the app or by email.

Contact

Questions or requests: support@libriq.app.
