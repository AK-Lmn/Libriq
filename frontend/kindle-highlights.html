Import your Kindle highlights

Every time you highlight a passage or add a note on a Kindle, it is written to a plain text file on the device called My Clippings.txt. LibriQ can read that file and save each highlight and note as a quote on the book it came from, so the lines you marked live next to your progress and your own notes.

Find My Clippings.txt

  1. Connect your Kindle to a computer with a USB cable.
  2. Open the Kindle drive and the documents folder.
  3. Copy My Clippings.txt somewhere easy to find.

The file covers every book you have highlighted on that Kindle, so one import can bring in years of reading at once.

Import it into LibriQ

  1. Open LibriQ and go to Settings, then Export / Import.
  2. Choose Import Kindle Clippings and select the file.
  3. Check the preview, then confirm.

The preview is read-only. It lists the books and clippings LibriQ found before anything changes in your library.

What gets imported

  • Highlights become quotes on the matching book.
  • Notes you typed on the Kindle come across too.
  • Page numbers are taken from the location Kindle records, so you can find the passage again.

Bookmarks are skipped, since they don't contain any text. The file is read in your browser and only the clippings you import are saved to your account.

Reading in the Kindle app instead?

My Clippings.txt only exists on Kindle e-readers. The Kindle apps for phones, tablets and computers keep highlights in your Amazon account instead and don't create this file, so this import works with highlights made on an e-reader.

Using your quotes

Quotes show on each book's details page alongside your notes and rating. They sync with the rest of your library between your devices, and they are included when you export a backup from Settings.

Coming from Goodreads?

Import your Goodreads library first, then your clippings. The highlights will land on the books you already brought across.
